This formative assessment, specifically Part II., analyzes the heights of college students using descriptive statistics and normal distribution. The data collected through google forms from the survey contains 61 responses, exceeding the target sample size of 50 respondents.


After the data collection, We used RStudio to read the excel file and calculated the mean, standard deciation and range. the group obtained a mean of 163.23 cm and a standard deviation of 10.67, having a height range of 149 cm to 188 cm. Furthermore, the researchers used frequency distribution, the empirical rule, and normal distribution curve to further analyze and interpret college student heights.

The results suggest that the data can be examined using an approximately bell-shaped normal distribution. The findings may also be used for practical applications such as apparel sizing, facility safety, laboratory workstations, and classroom furniture.
